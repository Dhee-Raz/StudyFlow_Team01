Get your personal access token

  1. Open your school’s Canvas website and sign in.
  2. Go to Account → Settings → Approved Integrations.
  3. Select New Access Token, enter “StudyFlow” as its purpose, and choose an expiration date.
  4. Generate the token and paste it here. Never share it in chat or commit it to GitHub.

If your school disables personal tokens, contact its Canvas administrator.

Disconnecting removes the token from StudyFlow. To revoke it in Canvas too, remove it under Approved Integrations.
